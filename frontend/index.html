Where puzzles
meet solutions.

Step-by-step guides for the Rubik's Cube, Sudoku and five other classic puzzles, with their history, common variations and links to solvers and timers.

Browse the categories

Source code on GitHub

SolveBox has guides for seven puzzles: the Rubik's Cube, Sudoku, crosswords, jigsaw puzzles, the Tower of Hanoi, the 15 puzzle and logic grid puzzles.

SolveBox explains how classic puzzles work and how to solve them.

Each guide starts with quick tips and a short fact, then covers the puzzle's history, video tutorials, advanced tips and common variations. Every page is available in English and Serbian.

Key categories

Every puzzle gets the same kind of guide, from first moves to advanced tips.

  • Quick tips and advanced tips;

  • History, facts and variations;

  • Video tutorials and links to solvers.

Rubik's Cube

A 3D combination puzzle: turn the faces until each side is one colour. The guide covers the standard cube and its 2x2, 4x4 and 5x5 versions.

Sudoku

Fill a 9x9 grid so that every row, column and 3x3 box holds the digits 1 to 9. The guide covers solving techniques and common variations.

Other Puzzles

Crosswords, jigsaw puzzles, the Tower of Hanoi, the 15 puzzle and logic grid puzzles each have a guide of their own.
